3-Phase Tasting Protocol

Hari's recommended method for every new cup. Use this on your next visit to Nylon, FLUID or Alchemist.

PHASE 1 — HOT (85–92°C)

Look for: Volatile top notes — florals (linalool), citrus (citric acid), herbal terpenes (thymol).

Vocabulary to use: "Jasmine forward", "bright citric zip", "thyme coating on palate"

Hari note: High temp unlocks esters. Record immediately.

PHASE 2 — MID (65–75°C)

Look for: Body & structure — malic acid (apple), anthocyanins (berry), sucrose caramelisation (toffee).

Vocabulary to use: "Round malic body", "jammy mid-palate", "persistent lipid finish"

Hari note: Temperature shift reveals hidden layers. Stir gently.

PHASE 3 — COOL (40–55°C)

Look for: Aftertaste & fade — lingering terpenes, residual sugars, low tannin clean finish.

Vocabulary to use: "Clean transparent fade", "honeyed aftertaste", "non-astringent finish"

Hari note: The true test of quality. Great coffees evolve beautifully as they cool.
