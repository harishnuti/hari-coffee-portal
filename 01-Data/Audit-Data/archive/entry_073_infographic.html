◈ The variable that made this cup — brewer material
Every prior V60 in this archive has been ceramic. This one is stainless steel: lower thermal mass, higher conductivity, so the slurry sheds heat faster through the brew. That compresses the usual florals-first, sugars-later sequence — which is precisely why sweetness turned up alongside the jasmine in the hot phase instead of waiting for the cool one.

Logged as Extraction Law 7: brewer material is a live variable, not a footnote.
