Pocket by Flip — Ecuador La Papaya

Singapore • 19 May 2026
93.5/100

Origin: Ecuador, Loja Province (1,850 MASL)

Varietal: La Papaya (Geisha × SL-28 hybrid)

Process: Washed, 36-hour fermentation

Producer: Finca La Papaya

Importer: The Coffee Collective

Tasting Notes
Peach • Jasmine • Bergamot • Silky mouthfeel • Long floral finish
"A masterclass in clarity and florality. The La Papaya shines with explosive aromatics and a refined, tea-like body. One of the standout lots of 2026."
Technical Note: Despite high altitude, the washed process preserved delicate florals without over-fermentation. Recommend 1:16.5 brew ratio for filter to highlight acidity. Best enjoyed 10–14 days post-roast.
