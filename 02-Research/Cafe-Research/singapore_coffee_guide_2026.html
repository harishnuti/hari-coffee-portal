SECTION 3 — SEASONAL LOTS CALENDAR (May 2026)

Ethiopia
Main harvest Oct–Feb 2026. Fresh crop arriving Feb–June. High cherry prices, tighter washed volumes. Peak now: Guji/Yirgacheffe washed & naturals. Target: Banko Gotiti style heirlooms.
Colombia
Main crop (fly crop) arriving Jan–March. Good washed lots available. Target: Sudan Rume, Wush Wush from Cauca/Quindío.
Panama
Geisha auction lots still circulating. High-altitude Chiriquí lots peak. Target: Janson Family or similar washed Geisha.
Ecuador
Harvest May–Sept. Loja high-altitude Geisha fresh. Target: 2100+ MASL terpene-forward lots (like La Papaya profile).
Kenya
Main crop Oct–Jan, exports Feb–March. AA lots strong now. Target: Nyeri/Kirinyaga washed for citric clarity.

SECTION 5 — HARI'S AUDIT PRIORITY LIST (Top 10 Unvisited)

Aligned to washed + Geisha/rare varietal + flat-burr/flat-bottom + transparent rotation

1. Exposure Therapy Coffee — Top-ranked 2026 roaster. Ultralight Ethiopian/Kenyan anaerobics. Target: Current Geisha or experimental washed. Visit: Weekday mornings. Distance from Everton Park: ~8km.
2. Aera Experiments — Ultralight specialist, expressive Panamanian/Colombian Geisha. Target: High-ferment or altitude lots. Hardware: Precision flat-bottom. Visit: Late afternoon.
3. Homeground Coffee Roasters — Community favourite for light, precise roasts. Strong single-origin focus. Target: Current Colombia/Ethiopia washed. Flat-burr setup likely.
4. Percolate.sg — International roasters focus. Excellent for rare lots. Target: Any current Geisha or Sudan Rume equivalent.
5. 20Grams Coffee Roastery — Light, precise. Strong 2026 buzz. Target: Light-roasted washed lots with high clarity.
6. Compound Coffee — Newer standout, community favourite. Target: In-house or international washed with flat-bottom hardware.
