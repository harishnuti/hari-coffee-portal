05 // FLAVOR LABORATORY

The Chemistry of Taste

Acid profiles · Flavor clusters · Hardware intelligence
Citric Acid
Zippy, bright, lemon-forward. Perceived most intensely hot. Primary acid in high-altitude washed Ethiopians and young anaerobic naturals.
Detected in: #026 (Banko Gotiti hot phase), #022 (La Florida hot), #005 (Subko light roast)
Malic Acid
Juicy, stone-fruit, apple-like. Perceived as cup cools. Dominant in Gesha, Typica Mejorado, and high-altitude naturals with intact cell structure.
Detected in: #017 (#022 cool phase), #024 (FAF green apple), #014 (Beloya plum)
Phosphoric Acid
Clean brightness without sharpness. Blackcurrant, plum-skin. Characteristic of Kenyan SL28/SL34 — not metallic but distinctly mineral and juicy.
Detected in: #004 (Kiamugumo AA), #012 (Ndiaini AA), #023 (Santuário Sur)
Acetic / Lactic Acid
Savory, fermented, complex. Acetic → vinegar-adjacent; lactic → creamy, yogurt-like. Signatures of deep anaerobic fermentation and extended natural processing.
Detected in: #006 (Raspberry in Loop acetic), #027 (Buena Vista savory), #009 (Masaguara lactic)

Grinder Intelligence

Mahlkönig EK43
98mm flat burrs · High RPM · Low fines
Workhorse of the audit. 98mm flat burr geometry delivers bimodal grind distribution, reducing fines that generate bitterness. Seen at Agora, Asylum, Tiong Hoe. Ideal for showcasing origin clarity in washed and natural filter coffees.
Mahlkönig EK Omnia
98mm flat burrs · Variable RPM · Precision
Paired with Nylon's xBloom flat-bottom brewer. The Omnia's stepped grind adjustment and low RPM option preserves volatile aromatics. Responsible for the bergamot clarity in Banko Gotiti and the crisp malic front on FAF Organic.
Timemore Sculptor 078
SSP 98mm flat burrs · Prosumer
Paired with Maxi Coffee Bar's flat-bottom brewer for La Laja Sudan Rume. SSP-modified burrs provide exceptional sweetness amplification — the nectarine and raspberry clarity achieved was a technical milestone.
