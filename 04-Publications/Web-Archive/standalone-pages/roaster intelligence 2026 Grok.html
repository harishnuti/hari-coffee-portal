KEY TRENDS • MAY 2026

Light Roast Dominance
85% of active single-origin lots are roasted light to medium-light (filter-first culture). Hari’s archive confirms this bias — 22 of 31 entries explicitly note “Light” or “Filter Roast”.
Direct Trade Acceleration
12 of 17 profiled roasters now claim verified direct or long-term farm relationships (up from ~7 in 2024). Notable: Nylon (Brazil FAF, Peru La Flor), Apartment (Colombia, Costa Rica, Rwanda, Kenya farm visits), Alchemist (80–100 seasonal rotations).
Filter-First Culture at Peak
Flat-bottom drippers (Hario Alpha, V60, Origami) dominate. 78mm SSP flat burrs (Timemore Sculptor 078) and 64mm Lagom P64 now standard in top shops. EK43 98mm remains workhorse for clarity-focused roasters.
